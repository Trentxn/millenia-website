# PRODUCT.md

register: brand

## Product purpose

Single page cinematic landing site for Millenia (full legal name: The Millenia Movement), a planned youth enrichment and community facility in Nassau, The Bahamas. Sport first (basketball), plus mentorship, conditioning, recovery, academics. The site is a vision piece: the business plan is in development, all offerings are intentional placeholders written with conviction.

## Users

Bahamian youth and their families, athletes, community members, and future partners and sponsors evaluating the vision. Mostly mobile, mixed network quality.

## Brand voice

Three physical words: poured concrete, court tape, buzzer. Bold, kinetic, confident. The energy of a basketball brand film translated into typography and motion. Plain strong verbs, short sentences, Bahamian pride, specific over clever. Speak to young people and families with respect.

## Anti references

Generic AI landing pages, community center brochures, SaaS templates, glassmorphism, card grids, centered hero with two buttons, stock metaphor copy ("unlock your potential", "elevate your journey"). No purple. No emoji. No em dashes or hyphens in rendered copy.

## Strategic principles

- Black and gold executed with restraint: gold is scarce (under 10% of any viewport)
- One signature element: THE GOLD LINE, a continuous court line drawn through the page by scroll
- One unified motion language (narrative: Four Quarters), directed like a title sequence
- The page must know it is in Nassau: true color Bahamian light break, geography in the footer
- Brand display name is "Millenia"; "The Millenia Movement" appears once, in the footer
